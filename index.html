EcoGo: Sustainable Consumption Platform

My BSc thesis at the University of Pécs. EcoGo makes sustainability visible: you scan a product, see its CO₂ footprint and eco-score, and add it to an impact log that tracks your choices over time. A multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA) algorithm scores products on environmental impact.

Three pillars: barcode scanning for real-time eco-scores, a personal impact dashboard (CO₂ and plastic savings), and gamified challenges with milestones and badges.

Stack: React Native (Expo) mobile app, React.js web dashboard with an admin panel, and Supabase (PostgreSQL, REST APIs, JWT authentication with role-based access).

Result: 4.6 / 5 user satisfaction in real-world validation testing.

Next: ML-personalized scoring, blockchain-verified eco-data, a sustainable product marketplace and team challenges.
